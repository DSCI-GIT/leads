# SplatCap3D Lead Sourcing Notes

## Product Understanding

SplatCap3D is not another drone survey offer. The SC3D lead database is built around spaces that benefit from photorealistic, walkable 3D digital twins.

The offer is similar to DSCI because both businesses turn real-world conditions into usable visual/spatial data. It is different because SC3D is primarily selling:

- Interior/exterior spatial capture.
- Gaussian-splat digital twins.
- Hosted browser walkthroughs.
- Web embeds and shareable links.
- Optional annotations, measurement, snapshots, teaser videos and repeat captures.
- Sales, leasing, training, coordination, heritage, tourism and operations use cases.

## Qualification Model

SC3D fit is based on whether the account owns, sells, operates, markets or documents spaces where a walkable 3D twin can reduce friction or create value.

High-fit signals:

- Valuable or visually important spaces.
- Spaces sold, leased, rented, booked or promoted online.
- Buyers, planners or stakeholders who need remote previews.
- Facilities where training or orientation is repeated.
- Venues, exhibits, showrooms, model homes or production locations.
- Active sites where repeat capture creates a useful timeline.
- Need for embedded walkthroughs, measurements, annotations or shareable links.

Lower-fit signals:

- Pure back-office service companies with no meaningful physical space to show.
- Accounts where the decision is mostly price-based commodity work.
- Spaces that are confidential, inaccessible or unlikely to allow visual publication.

## Difference From DS Lead Criteria

DSCI lead fit favors engineering, surveying, aerial mapping, inspection, LiDAR, construction documentation and infrastructure field capture.

SC3D lead fit favors sales enablement, spatial storytelling, remote walkthroughs, web-hosted digital twins, site familiarity, training and stakeholder communication.

The same company can belong in both databases, but the qualification and outreach angle should be different.
